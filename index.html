      How these stats were made
      • Source: the league's public pages on page.spordle.com: score sheets (goals, assists, penalties, lineups), the Lions schedule and roster, the Junior C standings and suspensions. Updated 24 November 2026.
      • Game 7075 (16 Nov vs Pierrefonds): Giacomo Mazzuca is credited with the second assist on the Lions' first goal (Arcoraci, 6:45 of the 1st). The league's online sheet lists only Struffolino; the assist was added at the team's request, so Mazzuca's totals here are one assist higher than the league's.
      • Game 7091 (23 Nov at Étoiles): corrected at the team's request. Antony Oustimenko scored the goals at 1:43 of the 1st and 1:10 of the 3rd (the league's sheet lists Choueiri and Florio), Xavier Choueiri has the assist on Somers' goal at 9:44 of the 1st, Oustimenko's listed assist on Somers' goal at 9:28 of the 3rd is removed, Reno Cariglia scored the goal at 9:07 of the 3rd (sheet: Somers), and Cariglia, not Somers, assisted on Oustimenko's goal at 11:27 of the 1st.
      • Game 7087 (22 Nov at West-Island): Jonathan Tomarelli's match penalty and major for physical abuse of an official count 5 minutes each, as the league's standings do. Bench staff penalties are left out of the player tables.
      • League tab: scoring leaders and penalty minutes were tallied from the league's online score sheets for 87 of the 89 games played, with games played taken from each sheet's lineup. Not yet included (no score sheet posted): game 7050 (Phénix vs Chevaliers, 2 Nov). Games still missing a posted sheet are added automatically once the league posts them. Penalty minutes count a minor as 2, a major as 5 and a misconduct as 10; team totals land within a few minutes of the standings. Team per-game figures come from the standings.
      • Goals for and against and the Lions' penalty minutes are checked against the league's standings.
      • Power-play and shorthanded goals are estimated: a goal counts as a power-play goal when the other team had more players in the box at that moment, with every minor taken as 2:00 and ended early by a power-play goal. Matching minors don't count as power-play chances.
      • Some players wore different numbers in the first game (Sciotto #1, Oustimenko #20, Choueiri #38); stats are matched by name and shown with roster numbers.
      • Dylan Lussier (listed "JA", an affiliated player) dressed once, on Oct 24.
      • Game 7010 lists the same head-contact minor twice for Noah Tyler Florio and Francis Daoust at 14:16 of the 3rd. The league counts both, so this page does too.
      • Game 7001 credits a goal to Braves du Nord goalie Konstantinos Nikolopoulos; that is how the sheet reads.
      • The official PDF game sheets for the first six games were downloaded alongside this data.
